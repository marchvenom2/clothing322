Shipping Policy

Effective Date: March 22, 2026

1. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days (excluding weekends and holidays). You will receive a confirmation email once your order has been dispatched.

2. Delivery Estimates

RegionStandard ShippingExpress Shipping
Domestic (US)3–7 business days1–3 business days
Canada5–10 business days3–5 business days

Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to holidays, customs processing, weather, or carrier delays.

3. Free Shipping

We offer free standard shipping on all domestic orders over $79. This offer applies automatically at checkout.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number and link. You can use this to monitor delivery progress in real time.

5. Shipping Restrictions

We currently ship to the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, European Union, and Australia. We do not ship to P.O. boxes for express orders. Additional duties or import taxes for international orders are the customer's responsibility.

6. Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package is marked as delivered but you have not received it, or if tracking shows no updates for more than 7 business days, contact us at support@velvetmuse.com and we will investigate with the carrier and work toward a resolution.
